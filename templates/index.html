      YouTube cookies
      
      
      
        Needed when YouTube blocks this server ("confirm you're not a bot") or for
        age-restricted, private or members-only videos. New cookies work immediately.
        Their contents are never shown or logged.
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
        
        
        
      
      
      
      
      
      
        How do I get a cookies.txt file?
        
        
          
      1. Open a private/incognito window and sign in to YouTube. A spare Google account is safest.
        2. 
          
      2. Use a "cookies.txt" export extension (for example "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY") on youtube.com to export the cookies.
        3. 
          
      3. Close the private window straight away, so YouTube doesn't replace those cookies, then paste or upload the file here.

      yt-dlp
      
      
      
        YouTube changes often, and old yt-dlp versions stop working. This installs the
        latest yt-dlp[default,deno], then restarts the app once no download is running.
        Finished files that haven't been saved yet are cleared by the restart.
